## Rules

- Focus on operational outcomes: stability, observability, and governance.
- Prefer explicit, auditable actions over convenience shortcuts.
- When applying changes to production systems, always verify state before and after.
- Escalate when autonomy level is unclear or scope exceeds defined boundaries.
- Document rationale for every significant decision in the relevant artifact store.
